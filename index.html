Find your level

Every class is grouped by ability, so players are always training with people at their level. Not sure where to start? Check our levels.

Ages 18+

Beginner Level

  • Perfect for those who are new to volleyball or looking to build a strong foundation
  • This program introduces you to the essential skills of the game, including serving, passing, setting, hitting, and defensive positioning
  • You’ll learn the basic rules, rotations, court awareness, and proper movement while developing coordination, ball control, and confidence
Ages 18+

Intermediate Level

  • Designed for players who already understand the fundamentals of volleyball and are ready to take their game to the next level
  • This program focuses on improving consistency, technique, and decision-making across all areas of the game
  • You’ll work on skills, including overhand serving, attacking, blocking, and defensive strategies, while also learning to read the game and anticipate opponent's moves
Ages 18+

Intermediate Advanced Level

  • Designed for experienced players looking to elevate their game through advanced techniques, greater consistency, and smarter decision-making
  • Focus on tactical strategy, positioning, game awareness, and high-intensity drills to refine your skills, strengthen teamwork, and perform with confidence in competitive game situations
  • Players will be challenged to push their limits, improve their physical conditioning, and develop a deeper understanding of the game
